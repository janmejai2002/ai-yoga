SPEAKER 1 — Janmejai (continued). Why does this matter now? B2B buying has moved online. Gartner expected most B2B sales interactions to be digital by 2025, so almost every decision today has a tool somewhere in it: a CRM, a pricing engine, a forecast, a scorecard. But research doesn’t agree on how people treat these tools. One stream shows people drop an algorithm the moment they see it make a mistake. Another shows people actually prefer algorithmic advice to a human’s. Both come from controlled experiments. Neither tells us what happens inside a real organisation, with a boss, a budget and a deadline. So we asked one question: when the system says one thing and experience says another, what do managers actually do, and why? And note what we did not study. We didn’t ask whether firms adopt AI. We studied judgment. We held [N] structured conversations across [N] industries, and for every story we pinned down the same six things: the context, the people, the trigger, the constraints, the options, and the outcome.
