In brief, across six decision episodes in six sectors we found that managers
rarely obeyed or ignored their tools outright; they negotiated with them. Two
factors - personal accountability if a decision fails and tacit context the system
cannot see - explain four decision logics (Shield, Argue, Filter and Override), with
clear implications for how B2B offerings are positioned and sold.
